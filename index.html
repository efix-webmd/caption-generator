Turn any video or audio file into timed captions.

Upload a file, generate captions, then export them as SRT, VTT or plain text. Audio is extracted in your browser, so the video itself never leaves your device.

Use your own Gemini API key (optional)

Sent only to Google's Gemini API. Get a key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. A key entered here is used instead of the built-in one.
